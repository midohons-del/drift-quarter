From Browser Tab to Obsession: The Drift Quarter Story

How Drift Quarter grew from a small games list into a destination players actually bookmark.

Drift Quarter started the way most good game portals do — as a short list of favorites, playable without downloads, shared between people who just wanted something quick to play between tasks. The library has grown a lot since then, but the original goal hasn't changed.

What's different now is the depth. Categories like Shooter have real curation behind them instead of just a pile of links, and titles like Sniper Circuit have become the kind of games players specifically come back to check on, not just stumble into.

The plan going forward is more of the same: keep the load times fast, keep the curation honest, and keep adding games that earn their spot instead of just filling space. If Drift Quarter is doing its job, it should feel less like a directory and more like a place you actually want to open.
